# Research method and conclusion boundaries

The core unit is an explicit hypothesis, not a scalar market-state label.
The included rule file is a synthetic demonstration of conditional reasoning,
with arbitrary test thresholds rather than empirically calibrated economics.

## Hypothesis specification

A hypothesis records its question, proposed mechanism, scoped metal and geography,
version, declared effective time, alternatives, conclusion limits, and material
versus contextual evidence requirements. The full definition is hashed into every report.

The illustrative question concerns tighter readily deliverable copper conditions.
It does not equate exchange stocks with all inventory, imports with consumption,
production with demand, or calendar spreads with an identified physical shortage.

## Evidence is not quality

- **SUPPORTS:** comparable observations move in the rule's modeled expected direction.
- **WEAKENS:** comparable observations move against that expected direction.
- **INSUFFICIENT:** no directional interpretation is justified because the series is
  contextual, neutral, stale, suspect, incomplete, or semantically incompatible.
- **UNAVAILABLE:** no eligible observation exists at the local knowledge cutoff.
- **Material conflict:** supporting and weakening material evidence coexist; report
  `INCONCLUSIVE` rather than asserting that physical conditions are tightening.

`PRELIMINARY` and `REVISED` describe the observation, not its economic direction.
`HIGH`, `MODERATE`, and `LOW` quality are deterministic operational summaries of
required inputs, not calibrated confidence or probabilities. Optional absent
shipments remain visibly unavailable even if they do not prevent a scoped conclusion.

## Why imports remain contextual

The same import change can fit multiple proposed mechanisms in this model:
changes in demand, local supply, location, timing, or relative prices. The engine
therefore refuses to turn import direction alone into evidence for consumption or
tightness; a narrower mechanism and additional observations would be required.

Production is directional only under this demo's explicit supply-side,
all-else-equal hypothesis. A rise in production does not establish weaker demand
or disprove tightness when unmeasured demand could have risen further.

## Research discipline

The mechanism and threshold choices are not validated by passing software tests.
Tests establish implementation invariants, while economic usefulness would require
separate documented cases, coverage review, sensitivity analysis, and holdout periods.
Do not present replay correctness as causal evidence or investment performance.

Only one hypothesis is included. Do not expand to trading, portfolio, ML,
dashboards, or many additional datasets before one real-data case can be supported
with honest provenance and explicit limitations.
